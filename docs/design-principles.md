Distinctive thread: "dispatch console at dawn" - a field-ops tool for lawn and property crews. Deep navy command surfaces (headers, hero stat panels, bottom nav) sit over a cool, almost-white work surface. One electric signal: a blue-to-cyan gradient (#0C6FD0 -> #35C6F4) used only for the primary action, the selected state, and live metrics. Everything else is disciplined slate neutrals tinted toward blue.
Mode: light-only. The navy is structural (headers/hero bands), not a dark theme.
Pacing: tight. Mobile-first, thumb-reachable. Dense lists of cards, 16px card radius, hairline #DCE7F1 borders, no heavy shadows on list cards; soft glow shadow only on the primary gradient elements.
Type: Plus Jakarta Sans for everything (800 weight, -0.02em tracking for headings and big numerals). IBM Plex Mono in tiny uppercase tracked labels (dates, job numbers, section eyebrows) - the "instrument" accent.
Status is carried by small pill badges with a tinted background + saturated text (amber in progress, green done/paid, blue scheduled, red cancelled). Never colour whole cards by status.
Motion: short rise-in on cards (14px, 0.4s), bottom sheets slide up, press-scale 0.97 on tappable cards.
